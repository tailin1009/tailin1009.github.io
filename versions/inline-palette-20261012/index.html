About Me

I am a researcher in computer vision and multimodal learning. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Newcastle University, supervised by Prof. Yu Guan. My research spans skeleton-based action recognition, few-shot learning and multimodal video understanding, with research experience at the University of Exeter, ShanghaiTech University and Baidu.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal video understandingHate detection and temporal localisation.
  • Human action recognitionSkeleton-based modelling and spatio-temporal learning.
  • Few-shot learningTransferable representations and part-aware prototypes.
